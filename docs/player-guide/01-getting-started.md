# Getting Started with TalesMUD

Welcome to TalesMUD! This guide will help you take your first steps into the game world.

## Your First Login

When you first connect to TalesMUD, you'll need to:

1. **Create or Select Your Character** - Choose from your existing characters or create a new one
2. **Choose Your Race and Class** - Select the character attributes that fit your playstyle
3. **Enter the Game World** - You'll start in the game's starting area

## Understanding the Interface

The TalesMUD client has several main components:

### The Room Panel
- Shows the **current room's image** (pixelated background)
- Displays the **room name** at the top
- Shows the **room description** with details about your surroundings
- Lists **NPCs and creatures** present in the room

### The Terminal
- Classic MUD-style text interface
- Shows game messages and responses to your commands
- Type commands directly into the terminal
- Press **Up/Down arrows** to cycle through command history

### The Action Bar
- **Direction buttons** (North, East, South, West, Up, Down) for quick navigation
- **Standard commands** (Look, Inventory, Equipment, Character)
- **More button** for additional commands
- **Room-specific actions** when available

### Side Panels
- **Character** - Your stats, health, experience, and gold
- **Inventory** - Items you're carrying
- **Equipment** - Gear you have equipped
- **Quest Log** - Your active and completed quests
- **Minimap** - Shows explored areas

## Basic Navigation

### Moving Around

You can move in several ways:

1. **Click direction buttons** in the Action Bar (N, E, S, W, Up, Down)
2. **Type direction names** in the terminal: `north`, `south`, `east`, `west`, `up`, `down`
3. **Use shortcuts**: `n`, `s`, `e`, `w`, `u`, `d`

Only exits that are available from your current room will be shown as buttons.

### Looking Around

- **`look`** - Re-examine the current room and see everything around you
- **`examine [object]`** - Look closely at something specific

## Basic Commands

Here are the essential commands every player should know:

| Command | Shortcut | Description |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| `look` | `l` | Look at the current room |
| `inventory` | `i` | Check what you're carrying |
| `equipment` | `eq` | See what gear you have equipped |
| `character` | `c` | View your character stats |
| `who` | - | See who else is online |
| `help` | `?` | Get help information |

## Your First Quest

1. **Look around** for NPCs (non-player characters)
2. **Talk to NPCs** by clicking the blue "Talk" button on their card or typing `talk [npc name]`
3. **Accept quests** by selecting conversation options (type the number)
4. **Check your quest log** to see objectives
5. **Complete objectives** and return to the quest giver

## Picking Up Items

When you see items on the ground:

1. Look for the **Pickup** button in the Action Bar
2. Click it to see a list of items available
3. Select the item you want to pick up

Or type: `pickup [item name]`

## Combat Basics

When you encounter enemies:

1. Click the **Attack** button on the enemy's card, or type `attack [enemy name]`
2. During combat, you'll see new buttons: **Attack**, **Defend**, **Flee**
3. Your character widget will glow red when you're in combat
4. Use **Attack** to deal damage, **Defend** to reduce damage taken, or **Flee** to escape
5. Victory gives you experience points and possibly loot!

## Tips for New Players

- **Read room descriptions** - They often contain important clues
- **Talk to everyone** - NPCs may have quests or useful information
- **Check your minimap** - It helps you avoid getting lost
- **Save your gold** - You'll need it for better equipment
- **Complete quests** - They give great rewards and experience
- **Explore thoroughly** - You never know what you'll find
- **Use the terminal** - Some features are faster via text commands

## Customizing Your Layout

You can customize the game interface:

1. Click the **Edit Layout** button (usually in the top-right)
2. **Drag widgets** to reposition them
3. **Resize widgets** by dragging their edges
4. **Add or remove widgets** using the widget menu
5. Click **Save Layout** when you're done

Your layout preferences are saved automatically.
